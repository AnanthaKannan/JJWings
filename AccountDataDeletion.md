JJ Brain Wings Abacus ("the App") allows admins, teachers, and students to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account and data, please email us at: sreeananthakannan@gmail.com

Include the following in your request:

Your registered username or email ID
Your role (Admin / Teacher / Student)
The organization/coaching center name (if applicable)

If you are a parent or guardian requesting deletion of a student's data, please mention this in your email along with the student's registered details.

What Gets Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, the following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted from our systems:

Profile information (name, username,  profile photo)
Login credentials
Activity and progress data within the App
What May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period where required for legitimate business or legal purposes, such as:

Records needed to comply with applicable law
Aggregated, anonymized data that no longer identifies you
Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification.

Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, contact us at sreeananthakannan@gmail.com.
