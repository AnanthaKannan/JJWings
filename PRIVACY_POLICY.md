# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the JJBrainWIngsAbacus mobile application, package name `com.jjwings`, published by [sreeananthakannan@gmail.com] ("we", "our", or "us").

JJBrainWIngsAbacus is an educational app for students and administrators. It supports student login, homework and practice activities, progress tracking, notifications, messaging, profile photos, question paper uploads, and achievement content.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide and operate the app.

### Account and Profile Information

We may collect:

- Student name
- Student ID or login ID
- Password or authentication credentials
- Student level or class level
- Profile photo, if uploaded
- Admin account details, where applicable

### Learning and Activity Information

We may collect:

- Assigned homework and practice tasks
- Student answers and task completion status
- Scores, correct and incorrect answers, ranking data, and progress information
- Time taken or other learning performance information
- Messages exchanged between students and administrators
- Notifications sent through the app

### Device and App Information

We may collect:

- Device identifiers used to manage student access on the same device
- Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to send push notifications
- Basic app, device, and network information needed for login, security, and app functionality

### Files and Media

The app may allow users or administrators to upload:

- Profile photos
- Question papers, including PDF, document, or image files
- Achievement or celebration images

The app does not request access to the camera, contacts, microphone, SMS, call logs, or location through the Android manifest. If a user chooses a file or photo from the device, the selected file is used only for the related app feature.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create, manage, and authenticate student and admin accounts
- Show student homework, practice tasks, scores, rankings, and progress
- Allow administrators to manage students, questions, files, notifications, and messages
- Send push notifications about app activity, assignments, or announcements
- Display uploaded profile photos, question papers, and achievement images
- Keep the app secure and prevent unauthorized access
- Troubleshoot, maintain, and improve app functionality

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

We may share or process information with service providers that help us operate the app, including:

- Backend hosting and API services
- File storage services used for uploaded files
- Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
- App infrastructure, security, and maintenance providers

These providers process data only as needed to support app functionality. We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or our services.

## Data Storage and Security

The app sends data to our backend service using network connections. User credentials saved on the device are stored using the device keychain or secure credential storage where supported.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We retain user information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain learning records, support account access, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Users, parents or guardians, and authorized school or academy administrators may request correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us at [sreeananthakannan@gmail.com]. When an account deletion request is approved, we will delete or de-identify the related account information and associated personal data unless we are required or permitted to retain certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, backup, or legitimate administrative reasons.

If the app account was provided by a school, academy, tutor, or administrator, deletion requests may need to be verified through that organization or administrator.

## Push Notifications

The app may request permission to send push notifications. Push notification tokens are used only to deliver app-related notifications. Users can disable notifications through their device settings.

## Children and Students

JJBrainWIngsAbacus may be used by students, including students under the age of 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in their location. Student accounts should be created or managed by an authorized parent, guardian, teacher, academy, or administrator.

We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary for the educational features of the app. Parents, guardians, or authorized administrators may contact us to review, correct, or request deletion of a student's personal information.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services and SDKs, including Firebase services, for push notifications and app operation. These services may process device or app information as needed to provide their functionality.

Users can review Firebase privacy information at:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## User Choices

Users may:

- Stop using the app at any time
- Disable push notifications in device settings
- Delete saved login credentials by logging out where supported
- Request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us

## International Data Processing

Information may be processed and stored in countries where our service providers operate. By using the app, users understand that information may be processed outside their country of residence, subject to applicable law.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a policy update means the updated policy applies.
